## Context
v1 is live, with CR-013/014 deployed, the dark-mode check passed, and the opening imported on 2026-10-09. Your new feedback asks for three things:
- **Fixes:** the phone Back button with open sheets, the Journal "Who" alignment, dd/mm/yyyy fields, bank badges, and a card type field.
- **Look:** themes, and a less cluttered way to reach actions.

The code has no PDF, print, clipboard or share support today, no business profile, no date ranges on detail pages, and no invoice numbers. The Excel export is the whole set of books as of today.

PRD §5 lists "PDF/WhatsApp statements" as **v2**. So this becomes a **v1.1 scope**: one D entry, plus PRD/SRS/UIDD updates, before any code.

**Guardrail for everything below:** no posting rule or `computeBooks` change.
- `src/lib/engine/**` stays untouched (`git diff --stat src/lib/engine` must be empty).
- Every new number is read from the existing books and has a **tie-out test** against an existing total.
- All schema changes ship together in **one early migration (CR-016)**. They are additive, except the card-name backfill, which you approve row by row.
- **The UI stays simple:** see the simplicity rules below.

### UI simplicity rules (every CR is checked against these; a CR that breaks one is redesigned before it's built)
1. **Nothing new is always visible on a page** except the 2 main buttons + ⋯ on detail pages, and one ⤴ Share icon in the title row of report-type pages (Journal, Months, Cash book, Sales). Home gets nothing new.
2. **Occasional things live in sheets:** ⋯ More, the Report sheet, the send preview, bank accounts and card details. They open from one place, Back closes them (CR-015), and they look the same everywhere.
3. **Optional parts start closed:** "How to pay" is off; "Count cash" is a link that opens a small box; the statement's period chip only appears while a period is set.
4. **Short forms:** a field appears only when it's needed (as the entry forms already do). Settings → Business is split into small sections (Details · Logo · Messages), not one long form.
5. **One way to do each thing:** reports always go through the same Report sheet, and messages always through the same preview.
6. **Proof:** each CR ends with 360 px and 1280 px screenshots of every screen it touched, for your review, plus checks for no horizontal scroll, 44 px tap targets and axe-clean colours in all 3 themes.

**Order inside the migration:**
1. Drop the old unique (owner, name) **before** adding (owner, name, network). Otherwise renaming "EBL Visa" → "EBL" would clash with "EBL Master" → "EBL".
2. Backfill the numbers in (date, created_at) order, skipping reversals.
3. Then add the unique indexes and `setval` the sequences to the max.

**Code that must change with it (found in review):**
- **Reversals:** `reverse.ts` → `content()` (line 98) copies every header column into the reversal. It must also drop `invoiceNo` / `receiptNo`; otherwise reversing a sale would copy its number (a unique-key error).
- **Number assignment:** done in code, not as a column default, because reversals live in the same tables and must get none. An idempotent replay (`on conflict (id) do nothing`) may use up a number; that leaves a gap, which is accepted.
- **Export:** `src/server/export.ts` lists its tables by hand (`TABLES`). Add `business_profile` (without the `logo` column: Excel cells hold at most 32,767 characters) and `bank_accounts`. Never add `card_secrets` / `passkeys`.
- **Setup delete guard:** it treats every foreign key as "used" (`setup.ts:56`). It must ignore `bank_accounts` / `card_secrets` and delete them together with the owner.

**Checks:**
- **Safety:** every change is additive (new tables, or new columns with defaults). The only data change is the card rename below.
- **Tests:**
  - integration: the migration applies on a copy of the current schema;
  - every existing test stays green;
  - the numbers are sequential and unique when two sales are saved at once;
  - a reversal gets no number;
  - the export has no `card_secrets` / `passkeys` sheets.

  - **A customer in credit** (due below 0): "Advance: ৳X" instead of "Due now: -৳X".
  - **A sale line split over two lots:** stored as 2 sale lines, shown as **one** line (same model + price merged) in messages and PDFs.
  - **Number to send to:** the first number if the field holds several. Formats `01…`, `+880…` and `880…` are all accepted; anything else opens the share sheet.
  - **Very long text** (over ~1,500 characters; a long wa.me link can be cut on some phones): the preview offers Copy + Open chat instead.
  - **Times** are formatted for Asia/Dhaka explicitly. Your VPS runs on Singapore time (GMT+8), so the server's clock is never used for display.
  - **`{name}`** = the customer's proprietor or the person's name. If it's empty, the greeting drops the name ("Assalamu alaikum,").

### CR-026 — Card details behind a fingerprint (LAST; tables from CR-016)
- **Stored** (one row per card, `card_secrets`):
  - **cardholder name, full number, expiry, CVV**, each encrypted with AES-256-GCM (`node:crypto`);
  - the key `CARD_SECRET_KEY` (32 bytes) lives only in the server's `.env.local`. It is never in the DB, never `NEXT_PUBLIC`, and never in git;
  - only the **last 4 digits** are stored in plain text, so the card can be recognised while masked.
  - Edits are audited **without values** ("card details changed").
- **Audit:** `runAction` writes before/after automatically, so for `card_secrets` it is overridden to "card details changed" with no values.
- **Password fallback:** checked with a separate Supabase client that saves no session, so your current login isn't replaced. Failed attempts are rate-limited.
- **Responses:** reveal responses are sent with `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- **Backups:** pg_dump backups hold the numbers only in encrypted form; restoring them also needs the key.
- **Where:** card page ⋯ → Manage → **Card details**, and the **Cards tab** of **Saved details** (D-046: two tabs, Bank accounts · Cards; only cards with details are listed). **Edit** appears only while revealed (D-046). It shows `•••• •••• •••• 1234` (D-051: no expiry/CVV placeholders), with buttons Reveal / Edit.
- **Fingerprint** (passkey / WebAuthn, through `@simplewebauthn/server` + `/browser`):
  - **Settings → Fingerprint unlock → Set up on this phone** registers a passkey. Android Chrome asks for your fingerprint; Windows Hello / Touch ID work on a computer.
  - **Reveal:**
    1. the server issues a one-time challenge (valid 2 minutes);
    2. your fingerprint signs it;
    3. the server verifies the signature;
    4. it returns *only that card's* details.
  - The details show for **30 s** (`REVEAL_SECONDS = 30` in code), with ⧉ copy per field and Copy all, then mask again.
  - **No WhatsApp share** for card details.
  - **Fallback:** ~~if there's no passkey on the device, re-enter your login password~~ — none (D-052: a saved password could be autofilled). Without a fingerprint, Reveal links to Settings → Fingerprint unlock; the password is only asked at set-up.
  - Every reveal writes an audit row: who, which card, when — never the values.
- **Kept out of** the Excel export (no `card_secrets` / `passkeys` sheets), the audit history, logs and error messages. Integration tests assert this.
- **Risk you accepted (recorded in the D entry):**
  - the CVV is stored even though card rules (PCI) say not to;
  - the database and the server key **together** would expose the cards.

  Mitigations: encryption, the key apart from the DB, the fingerprint/password gate, the audit row on reveal, and no export.

  **Back up `CARD_SECRET_KEY`** (e.g. in your password manager). Without it, the stored numbers can't be read; `deploy/README.md` says so.
- **Tests:**
  - unit: encrypt/decrypt round trip; a wrong key or a tampered value fails;
  - integration:
    - reveal without a valid, fresh challenge or password is refused;
    - a reused challenge is refused;
    - the export has no secret data;
  - E2E: Playwright's virtual authenticator (Chromium) registers a passkey, then Reveal → 30 s → masked again.

### CR-031 — Supplementary card numbers + one fingerprint for 30 s (D-054; after CR-030)
- **Why:**
  - a card account can have a primary card and supplementary cards in other names, on one bill;
  - revealing the wrong card meant scanning the fingerprint again.
- **Several numbers per card.** `card_secrets.card_id` is no longer unique.
  - A new `supplementary` flag; at most one Primary per card (partial unique index).
  - Existing rows are Primary. The AAD stays the card id, so nothing is re-encrypted.
  - **Unchanged:** cards in Setup, pickers ("Arif · EBL Visa"), the engine, bills, reports, PDFs
    and WhatsApp. The swipe isn't recorded.
  - **UI:** each card once, its numbers under it ("Primary · •••• 1234", "Supplementary · ••••
    5678"). Add works on any active card, and the form has Primary / Supplementary chips.
- **Reveal window:**
  - a fingerprint reveal sets a signed `httpOnly` cookie (30 s, `Path=/cards`, `SameSite=Strict`;
    HMAC key derived from `CARD_SECRET_KEY`);

  - each reveal is audited (`via: fingerprint | window`).
- **Screenshots** can't be blocked from a web page. Hiding on blur keeps the details out of the
  app-switcher thumbnail.
